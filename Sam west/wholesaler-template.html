Common questions

Do you sell to individuals, or only registered businesses?

Both. Walk-in customers are welcome at bulk prices — you don't need a business registration to buy from the warehouse floor.

Can I get credit terms?

Credit accounts are available to businesses with a trading history with us, typically after the first few cash orders. New customers pay on collection or delivery.

What happens if an item is out of stock?

We'll flag substitutes of similar size and price where available, or hold the remainder of your order for the next delivery at no extra charge.

Do you deliver outside your listed counties?

Occasionally, for large orders — contact our sales line to check feasibility and delivery cost for your area.

A note on ordering safely

We only take orders through the channels listed below. If you're contacted by someone claiming to represent us on social media, or asked to pay a personal mobile number, treat it as a scam.

  • We never post prices or request payment through social media comments or DMs.
  • Delivery drivers do not collect cash — all payments go through our official paybill only.
  • If in doubt, call our sales line directly to confirm any order before paying.
